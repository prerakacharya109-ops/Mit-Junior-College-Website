Application Timeline

Follow our four-step process to enroll in your desired academic stream.

Step 01

Admission Enquiry

Fill out the online callback form on our website or visit the Alandi campus. Select your stream interest (Science or Commerce) and opt-in for competitive coaching (JEE/NEET/CET or CA/CS).

Step 02

Document Submission

Submit your Class 10th board marksheet, school leaving certificate, Aadhaar card copy, and passport size photographs for administrative verification.

Step 03

Stream & Coaching Selection

Meet with our expert academic counsellors to evaluate your career objectives (PCM / PCB engineering/medical options or Commerce CA/CS tracks) and choose the appropriate integrated program.

Step 04

Seat Confirmation

Receive your admission confirmation card. Secure your seat by completing the fee payment and submitting the physical documents at the college office.
