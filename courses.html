Our Academic Planning Systems

How we ensure students remain on target to secure top percentages and competitive selections.

calendar_month

Chapterwise Macroplanning

Before the term starts, a complete Chapterwise Macroplanning chart (e.g., Biology: 38 topics, 20 chapters) defines the exact sequence of chapters and lecture hours allocated to cover the entire board and entrance syllabus.

schedule

Daywise Microplanning

Lectures follow a rigorous Daywise Microplanning chart. Each day specifies the precise topic, sub-topic taught, class work examples, and specific textbook exercises allocated for homework.

assignment_turned_in

Saturday Weekly Exams

Continuous evaluation takes place through dedicated examinations on every Saturday. This ensures student preparedness for JEE/NEET/CET and CA/CS tests.

analytics

Digital Testing & Result Analytics System

MIT Junior Colleges, Alandi implements a multi-format Testing System matching the mode of competitive exams:

Offline Exams (OMR)

Pen paper exams are completed using standard JEE/NEET OMR answer sheets. Sheets are checked by OMR software to prevent manual errors.

Online Exams (CBT)

Computer-based practice exams are hosted on our dedicated exam portal (powered by Edutech), recreating the exact interface of JEE and CET.

Detailed Result Analysis

Students get unitwise score percentages, percentile rankings, question-wise time management reports, and results are emailed directly to parents.
