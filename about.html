Our Rich Scholastic Legacy

MAEER's MIT Group Pune has built a distinguished education legacy of more than 42 years. Our institutional network includes 4 universities and over 70 institutions, educating a combined cohort of 50,000+ active students, guided by 3,000+ expert faculty, and supported by a global community of over 100,000+ alumni.

At MIT Junior Colleges, Alandi, we extend this powerful legacy to junior college education (Class 11th and 12th). Located Opp. Gajanan Maharaj Temple, Alandi (D), our campus offers students a robust value-based academic environment designed to convert potential into high performance.

We blend the official HSC board curriculum with integrated coaching for entrance exams (JEE, NEET, MHT-CET, CA, and CS Foundation), preparing students not just for board exams, but for competitive success and rewarding careers.
